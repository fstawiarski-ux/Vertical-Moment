# Höllental pilot — files to provide

Upload files in small sector batches. Keep originals; do not rename them just
for this intake.

## Required for each sector

- **3D source:** preferred `.glb`; otherwise `.obj` + `.mtl` + every texture,
  or the original Blender/RealityCapture/MeshLab project.
- **Topo evidence:** guidebook route list and topo page/photo, with book title
  and page number if known.
- **Wall reference:** one clear wall photo, panorama or orthophoto used to
  align routes to the model.
- **Route facts:** anything missing from the route list: length, bolts,
  first-ascent details, projects, historical names, closures or warnings.
- **Location:** a GPX waypoint/track or an exact map point. Siegenfelder
  Steinbruch needs this specifically; the supplied Höllental GPX already has
  working points for the other three sectors.
- **Usage permission:** whether Vertical Moment may publish the image, topo,
  scan and text, and any required credit.
