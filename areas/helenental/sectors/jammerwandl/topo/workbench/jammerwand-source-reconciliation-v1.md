# Jammerwand source reconciliation — review draft

## Sources compared

- **Keltenkalk book topo:** 37 numbered routes.
- **Bergsteigen topo:** 36 numbered routes.

## Strong agreement

Routes 1–13 agree in sequence, names, and displayed grades apart from minor spelling/formatting differences. Both sources place the wall as a continuous left-to-right cliff with shared terrain around the central buttress and right-hand slab system.

## Decision rule for the redesigned topo

Keep **Keltenkalk’s 1–37 numbering** as the master sequence, because it retains one extra route and keeps the named sequence internally continuous. Mark Book 14 / web omission and Book 16 / web 15 as **field-verification items**. Do not publish French conversions, bolt counts, anchor information, or current grades from either source without on-site verification.
